About the college

Sri Aurobindo College began in 1996 as Sri Aurobindo First Grade College for Women, started by the Rajajinagar Education Society to bring quality higher education to the neighbourhood.

The college is built on the educational ideals of Sri Aurobindo and The Mother. Learning here is meant to go beyond marks: students are encouraged to think independently, take part in cultural and social life, and grow in confidence and character.

Today the degree unit offers three undergraduate programmes on a compact campus, where students get individual attention from their teachers.

Courses

Three full-time undergraduate degrees. Each one prepares you for a job, for professional exams, or for a master’s degree.

  • B.Com

    Bachelor of Commerce

    3 years, full time

    What you study

    • Financial and corporate accounting
    • Cost and management accounting
    • Income tax and GST
    • Business law and banking

    Where it leads

    • Accounts, audit and tax roles
    • Banking and financial services
    • A strong base for CA, CS and CMA
    • M.Com, MBA and further study

  • BBA

    Bachelor of Business Administration

    3 years, full time

    What you study

    • Principles of management
    • Marketing and human resources
    • Financial management
    • Entrepreneurship and business communication

    Where it leads

    • Management trainee roles
    • Sales, marketing and HR
    • Starting your own business
    • MBA and further study

  • BCA

    Bachelor of Computer Applications

    3 years, full time

    What you study

    • Programming in C, Java and Python
    • Data structures and databases
    • Web technologies
    • Networks and software engineering

    Where it leads

    • Software development and testing
    • Web and app development
    • IT support and networking
    • MCA and further study

Who can apply: students who have passed II PUC (10+2) or an equivalent exam from a recognised board. Admission is on merit in the qualifying exam.

Admissions

Four steps from application to your first day.

  1. Get the application form

    Download it from the college website or collect it from the college office.

  2. Submit it with your documents

    Attach copies of the documents listed below.

  3. Selection on merit

    Seats are given on the basis of marks in the qualifying exam.

  4. Confirm your seat

    Pay the fees and show your original documents for verification.

Documents to keep ready

  • SSLC (10th) marks card
  • II PUC (12th) marks card
  • Transfer certificate
  • Passport-size photographs
  • Aadhaar card copy
  • Migration certificate, if from another board
